Setup Wizard

This generates secrets.json by logging into your Google account inside an embedded browser. Nothing you type there is seen by this app itself; it happens directly inside Google's own pages.

idle
  1. Sign in to Google
  2. Fetch encryption keys
  3. Confirm everything works

Setting up the browser can take up to half a minute before anything appears below -- this is expected, not stuck. You will likely be asked to sign in to Google twice in a row (once for the account itself, once more for end-to-end-encrypted data) -- that is also expected, not a bug.
